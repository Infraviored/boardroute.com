# Privacy

**Short version:** boardroute runs in your browser. Your circuits stay on your device unless you sign in and save them, there are no ads, and without an account no cookies. The server keeps shortened access logs for two weeks and counts visits anonymously.

## Your circuits

The autorouter works entirely in your browser. Circuits, layouts and settings are stored in your browser's local storage and never sent to us. A share link contains the board inside the link itself (in the part after `#`, which browsers don't send to the server); it reaches only the people you give it to.

## Optional account

You can use boardroute without an account. If you sign in to save circuits, we store:

- your email address, when the account was created and when you last signed in;
- the circuits you save, including any part photos in them, and whether you shared them;
- a session cookie (`__Host-br_session`) that keeps you signed in for up to 180 days, and a temporary sign-in cookie (`__Host-br_signin`, 20 minutes) to prevent login CSRF. They are needed for the account and used for nothing else.

The sign-in link is sent by [Brevo](https://www.brevo.com) (Sendinblue SAS, Paris, France), which processes your email address on our behalf only to deliver that mail. A circuit you share can be opened by anyone who has its link, until you stop sharing it or delete it. Your IP address is used for a few minutes in memory to limit how many sign-in links can be requested; it is not stored. You can delete your account in the "My circuits" dialog at any time: that removes your address, all saved circuits and their links at once. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing the account you asked for).

## Server logs

When you load a page, our web server records the time, the requested address, the referring page, your browser's user-agent string and your IP address **with the last part removed** (for example `203.0.113.0`). We need this to run the site and to detect abuse. The logs are deleted automatically after 14 days. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in operating a secure website).

## Visitor statistics

We count visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com), hosted on our own server. It sets no cookies and stores no IP addresses: it records the page, the referring site, browser and operating system, screen size and the country, plus anonymous events such as "a layout was run". Nothing leaves our server. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## Fonts and other resources

All fonts, scripts and images are served from boardroute.com. No requests go to Google or other third parties.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, correction, deletion and restriction of your data, the right to object, and the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority. Since we can't link the shortened logs to a person, we hold no data about you unless you have an account. Contact: see the [imprint](/imprint/).
